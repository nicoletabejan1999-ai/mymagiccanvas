Privacy Policy

Last updated

We are a small shop. We need your name and address to send you a canvas, and your email to tell you it is on its way. That is the whole of it, unless you tick the optional advertising box. We do not sell your data to anybody, ever.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is , , registration number . Questions, requests and complaints go to and we answer within a month, usually the same week.

2. What we collect and why

When you order

Your name, email address, delivery address, the details of what you ordered and the personalisation you asked for — the names, the date, the font number and the ink colour numbers.

Why: to make your canvas, send it, and deal with you about it afterwards. Legal basis: performance of the contract between us. Without this data we cannot make or send anything.

When you pay

We never see or store your card number. Stripe collects it on its own page and tells us only that a payment succeeded, along with the last four digits, the card brand, the country and the name on the card, so we can match a payment to an order and handle refunds and disputes.

Legal basis: performance of the contract, and our legal obligation to keep accounting records. Stripe is an independent controller for its own purposes, under its privacy policy.

When you write to us

Your email address and whatever you tell us in the message. Legal basis: performance of the contract if it concerns an order, otherwise our legitimate interest in answering people who contact us.

When you visit the site

Our host records standard server logs — the pages requested, the time, the browser and a truncated network address — to keep the site up and spot abuse. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a working, secure site.

This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies. The configurator remembers one thing in your own browser (whether you dismissed a setup notice) and that never leaves your device.

The lettering fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser asks Google's servers for them and Google sees your network address in doing so. Google describes this in its privacy policy.

Advertising — only if you tick the box

At checkout there is an optional, unticked box asking whether we may use your details to improve our advertising on Meta's platforms, Facebook and Instagram. Your order goes through exactly the same whether you tick it or not, and nothing about your price, your delivery or your service changes.

If you do tick it, we may share a hashed form of your email address with Meta so it can recognise existing customers, avoid showing you adverts for something you have already bought, and find people with similar interests. Hashing means Meta receives a scrambled value rather than your address itself. We never send the personalisation text, the names on your canvas, or anything about who your guests are.

Legal basis: your consent. You may withdraw it at any time by emailing us, which is as easy as giving it, and withdrawing does not affect anything done beforehand.

As of today no Meta tracking pixel is installed on this site. If we add one, it will load only for visitors who have given consent, and this policy will be updated before it does.

A Meta pixel is active on this site. It loads only after consent has been given and records which pages a consenting visitor viewed and whether an order was completed, so we can measure whether an advert worked. Meta's handling of that data is covered by its privacy policy.

3. Who else sees your data

Only the people needed to get a canvas to your door:

  • Stripe — to take the payment.
  • Our carrier — your name, address and telephone number, so the parcel can be delivered and you can be reached about it.
  • Our host — , which serves this site and keeps the server logs described above.
  • Our email provider — to send and receive messages.
  • Our accountant and the tax authorities — invoices and payment records, because the law says we must keep them.
  • Meta — a hashed email address, and only if you ticked the advertising box.

We do not sell your data, rent it, or hand it to anybody for their own marketing.

4. Data leaving Europe

Some of the services above are United States companies, so your data may be processed outside the European Economic Area. Where that happens we rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, or on an adequacy decision where one covers the recipient.

5. How long we keep it

  • Order and invoice records — for as long as accounting and tax law requires, which is normally ten years from the end of the financial year.
  • Personalisation text — kept with the order for the same period, so we can reprint if a canvas is lost or damaged.
  • Emails — three years after our last exchange, unless they concern a dispute, in which case until it is closed.
  • Advertising consent — until you withdraw it, and we keep a record of the withdrawal so it is not asked of you again.
  • Server logs — a short period set by our host, and then deleted automatically.

6. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you may:

  • ask what we hold about you and get a copy;
  • have mistakes corrected;
  • have your data erased, where we are not obliged to keep it;
  • ask us to restrict what we do with it;
  • receive it in a portable, machine-readable form;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent to advertising at any moment.

Email and say which one you want. We do not charge for this and we do not ask why.

If you think we have handled your data badly, please tell us first so we can fix it. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority in the European country where you live or work.

7. Children

This shop is not aimed at children and we do not knowingly collect their data. Children do press their fingerprints onto these canvases at christenings and birthdays — but those prints stay on your canvas in your home. They never reach us.

8. Security

The site is served over an encrypted connection. Card details never touch our servers. Order records sit in accounts protected by strong passwords and two-factor authentication, and only the people who make and post your canvas can see them.

9. Changes

If we change how we handle data — for instance when we start running adverts — we update this page and change the date at the top before the change takes effect.
